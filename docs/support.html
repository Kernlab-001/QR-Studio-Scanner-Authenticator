Free forever · MIT licensed · no tracking, no cloud, no account

QR Studio never sends anything anywhere: every code you scan, every secret in your vault and every scan in your history stays on your device. It is free, open source and has no ads and no upsells. If it saved you a bit of time, you can chip in and keep it that way.

Ways to support the project

Monthly Sponsor on GitHub Any amount, cancel whenever you like. GitHub handles the payment and the receipt — I never see your card details. One-time Buy the Pro licence One payment, no subscription, no account. It unlocks bulk vault import and export, batch QR generation and scheduled encrypted backups. Scanning and the authenticator stay free for everyone, and the licence is verified offline, so the extension never contacts a server. Free Star the repository and share it Useful for exactly the same reason a donation is: more people using a local-only scanner keeps the good version the popular one.

What your support pays for

  • Keeping the extension free of ads, telemetry and dark patterns.
  • More browsers: Edge and Firefox submissions, and a build that works on phones and tablets.
  • Improvements to the parts that matter — a better decoder, faster scanning, more import formats.
  • Team and self-hosted features for people who want a shared vault, priced per seat.

What it will never be used for

There is no plan to add analytics, ad targeting, a login or a subscription to the core features. If a future version of QR Studio ever did any of those, it would be a different product — and the source of this one stays open so you can check.
