What your support pays for

  • Keeping the extension free of ads, telemetry and dark patterns.
  • More browsers: Edge and Firefox submissions, and a build that works on phones and tablets.
  • Improvements to the parts that matter — a better decoder, faster scanning, more import formats.
  • Team and self-hosted features for people who want a shared vault, priced per seat.

What it will never be used for

There is no plan to add analytics, ad targeting, a login or a subscription to the core features. If a future version of QR Studio ever did any of those, it would be a different product — and the source of this one stays open so you can check.
